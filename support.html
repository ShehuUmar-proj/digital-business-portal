Frequently Asked Questions

Registration & Account

How do I create an account?

To create an account, click on "Register Account" in the top navigation, fill in your email and password, and verify your email address. You'll then be able to start the business registration process.

What if I forgot my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email address, and we'll send you a password reset link within minutes.

Can I have multiple accounts?

You can have up to 3 active accounts with the same email address. Each account can manage one business registration.

How do I update my account information?

Go to your dashboard, click on Settings, and update your personal information. Changes take effect immediately.

Business Registration

How long does the registration process take?

Most registrations are completed within 2-3 business days after payment confirmation. You'll receive an email notification once your registration is approved.

What documents do I need to register?

Required documents include: National ID, Tax documents, Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum & Articles, and Proof of Address. See the dashboard for the complete list.

Can I save my progress and come back later?

Yes! Your registration progress is automatically saved. You can log out and resume at any step without losing your information.

How much does registration cost?

The registration fee is ₦2,500. This includes document verification and certification. No hidden charges.

Documents & Uploads

What file formats are accepted?

We accept PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, and DOCX files. Maximum file size is 10MB per document.

Can I delete documents after upload?

You can delete documents from your vault anytime, except those required for your current registration stage.

Is my document storage secure?

Yes, all documents are encrypted with military-grade encryption and stored on secure servers. Only you and authorized agents can access your documents.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept debit cards, bank transfers, USSD, and mobile wallets. All transactions are processed securely.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes, all payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We never store your card details.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available within 7 days of payment if your registration hasn't been processed. Contact support for details.
